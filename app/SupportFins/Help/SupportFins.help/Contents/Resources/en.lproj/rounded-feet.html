Rounded support feet

Rounded feet optionally rounds the corners of FDM bed-standing wall, squat, wedge and Sway feet inside their existing clearance envelope. It starts off, so existing geometry stays unchanged. Vertical/resin pin mode keeps its own windowed/undercut feet, even if the setting is saved as on. The choice is undoable and saved in 3MF sessions. Engine callers opt in with tunables: { roundFeet: true }; direct Sway callers use roundFeet: true. Omitted/false restores ordinary feet. Segment joints overlap inside the foot contour rather than meeting face to face. Rounding removes corner area; plate adhesion and tall-print stability have not been physically compared. Check the sliced first layers and print-test before using it for tall parts.
