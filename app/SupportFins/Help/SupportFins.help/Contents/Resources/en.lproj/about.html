About Support Fins

Credit

The fin technique is Slant3D's — they've evangelized designed-in supports for years. Support Fins just automates it. docs/FIN-SPEC.md cites their numbers directly.

License

MIT. The license covers this tool, not what you make with it — STLs you run through Support Fins are entirely yours, and the output carries no license obligation.

STEP import uses occt-import-js (Open CASCADE compiled to WebAssembly), vendored unmodified under web/vendor/occt-import-js-0.0.23/ with its LGPL-2.1 license files. The browser only downloads it when you go to import a file.

Pointer picks on dense parts use three-mesh-bvh, vendored unmodified under web/vendor/three-mesh-bvh-0.9.15/ with its MIT license.
